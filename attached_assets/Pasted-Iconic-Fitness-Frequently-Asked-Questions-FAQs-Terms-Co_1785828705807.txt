Iconic Fitness – Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) & Terms & Conditions

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

 1.⁠ ⁠What are your operating hours?

Clubs are open 5:00 AM – 11:00 PM, 365 days a year (may vary by location).

 2.⁠ ⁠Can I access all branches?

Yes, with an All Club Access membership you can use all participating branches.

 3.⁠ ⁠Are group classes included?

Yes, if included in your plan. Class availability and timings may vary.

 4.⁠ ⁠Can I pause my membership?

Yes, but only as per your plan terms and subject to management approval.

 5.⁠ ⁠Do you offer Personal Training and diet plans?

Yes. Certified trainers and diet consultations are available and may be included in select plans or charged separately.

 6.⁠ ⁠Are facilities like steam and showers available?

Available at selected branches only. Please check with your location.

 7.⁠ ⁠Can I transfer my membership?

Yes. Membership transfer is permitted subject to the following conditions:

•⁠  ⁠The membership can be transferred only to a non-member of Iconic Fitness.
•⁠  ⁠Existing Iconic Fitness members are not eligible to receive a transferred membership.
•⁠  ⁠Only the gym membership can be transferred. Personal Training packages, diet plans, promotional benefits, add-on services, merchandise, or any other packages are not transferable.
•⁠  ⁠A minimum of 60 days of active membership validity must be remaining at the time of the transfer request.
•⁠  ⁠The applicable membership transfer fee must be paid.
•⁠  ⁠All transfers are subject to management approval and verification.

 8.⁠ ⁠What should I bring to the gym?

Bring your app/QR code, workout attire, clean shoes, and a water bottle.

 9.⁠ ⁠How do I contact support?

Contact your branch directly or use the support option in the Iconic Fitness App.
