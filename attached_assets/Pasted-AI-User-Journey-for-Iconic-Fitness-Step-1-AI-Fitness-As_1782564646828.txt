Step 4: AI Assigns a Personal Trainer

After the assessment, the AI recommends a trainer based on the user's goals and health.

Examples:

Weight loss specialist
Muscle gain coach
Women's fitness trainer
Senior fitness coach
Rehabilitation trainer
Yoga trainer

The trainer receives the user's:

BMI
Fitness score
Goals
Medical conditions
AI-generated workout plan
Progress history

The trainer can review and adjust the AI plan before approving it.
