One Membership. Unlimited Gyms. Book any class — from sunrise yoga to midnight MMA. Your fitness journey, simplified.

1. What is Iconic Fitness?

Iconic Fitness is one of Bengaluru’s leading fitness chains offering premium gym facilities, certified personal training, group classes, and in-house diet consultations. Our mission is to help members transform their fitness safely and effectively through expert coaching and a supportive community.

6. Are personal trainers available?

Yes. Certified personal trainers are available at all branches. Personal training sessions and customised coaching packages are charged separately; ask your home branch for trainer rates and availability.

7. What are your group class timings?

Typical class slots: Morning: 7:00 AM – 8:00 AM; Evening: 7:00 PM – 8:00 PM. Classes run Monday to Saturday. Beginner-friendly classes and trial sessions are available—check the schedule in the app or at your branch.

8. Do you have a dietician?

Yes. Our in-house dietician provides personalised meal plans for weight loss, muscle gain, performance goals, and general nutrition guidance. Diet consultations are included once with membership; follow-up packages may be chargeable.

9. Can I cancel, transfer, or pause my membership?

Memberships are non-refundable. Transfers or pauses are allowed for valid reasons (medical issues, relocation, extended travel). Supporting documentation may be required—contact support to initiate a transfer or pause request.

10. What about hygiene & safety?

Regular sanitisation of equipment throughout the day

Daily cleanliness checks for locker rooms and showers

First-aid available at every branch

Daily fragrance and hygiene standards monitored by staff

11. What are your payment options?

We accept cash, cards, UPI, and online payments. For online payments, use our official payment link provided by staff at registration.

12. Do you have a mobile app?

Yes. Members can download the Iconic Fitness app to browse classes, view schedules, book sessions, and manage their membership. Check the Google Play Store or App Store by searching Iconic Fitness (or ask staff for the direct download link).
