1. Member Onboarding Master
Auto workflow after enrollment: Trainer Assignment, Welcome Call, Welcome WhatsApp Message
and Welcome Kit by Member Coordinator.
2. Initial Assessment Master
Within 24 hours, member visits on an empty stomach for BMI, Body Fat, Muscle Mass, Visceral Fat,
Water %, BMR, Weight, Photos (optional), Goals and Medical History.
3. Trial Session 1
Fitness Assessment including Mobility, Strength, Flexibility, Posture Analysis, Lifestyle Discussion,
Goal Setting and Assessment Report.
4. Session Feedback
Member rates Trainer Behaviour, Explanation, Knowledge, Friendliness, Confidence and provides
comments or complaints.
5. Trial Session 2
Gym Orientation covering Warm-up, Stretching, Equipment Usage, Machine Safety, Correct
Posture, Range of Motion, Breathing Technique, Workout Etiquette and Cool Down.
6. Session 2 Feedback
Collect ratings and comments after orientation.
7. PT Counselling Workflow
Trainer marks member ready. Member Coordinator explains PT Packages, Pricing, Offers and EMI.
Trainer explains transformation plan, accountability, nutrition support and progress tracking.
8. PT Decision
If PT is purchased: assign trainer, create PT schedule and enable PT dashboard. If not:
automatically start the General Member Engagement Program.
9. General Member Engagement
Assign a 45-day workout plan with Beginner, Intermediate or Advanced workout cards including
warm-up, workout, cardio, core and stretching.
10. Dietician Assignment
Automatically assign the member to the in-house dietician for diet planning, meal timing, hydration
and follow-up.
11. Follow-up Master
Automatic follow-ups on Day 7, Day 15, Day 30 and Day 45 to record attendance, motivation,
issues, improvement and PT interest.
12. Re-conversion Master
Automatic PT review reminders on Day 15, 30 and 45 for members who have not purchased PT.
13. Member Engagement Dashboard
Show onboarding progress, BMI, trial sessions, feedback, PT counselling, PT status, workout plan,
diet plan, attendance, last visit and next follow-up.
14. Trainer Dashboard
Track assigned members, pending BMI, pending sessions, feedback, PT conversion, workout
assignments, dietician referrals and follow-ups.
15. Automatic Notifications
Welcome, BMI reminder, Trial Session reminders, Feedback requests, PT counselling reminder,
Workout Plan, Diet Consultation and Day 15/30/45 progress reminders.
16. Member Engagement Score
Calculate an engagement score (0-100). Green: 80-100, Yellow: 60-79, Red: Below 60. Members
with low scores automatically appear in Trainer and Member Coordinator follow-up lists.
